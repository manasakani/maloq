# QHFlow3 vendor source

These modules are the minimal active fairchem math dependency set used by
`QHFlow3CleanFeatures`. They were copied from the `ml-dft` QHFlow2 legacy
vendor tree on 2026-07-21. The files retain their Meta MIT notices and are
covered by `LICENSES/MIT-fairchem.md` in this repository.

The QHFlow3 clean trunk itself comes from
`ml_dft.models.backbones.baselines.qhflow3_clean` and was adapted only at its
package boundaries and MALOQ batch/output bridge.
